## Strategy

**Who we're speaking to**
- **Older Muslims** who want to memorise or improve their recitation but can't easily get to a teacher.
- **Their children**, who often install apps for them.
- **People who know the Quran well** and could volunteer to teach.

**What sets Sanadi apart.** The closest alternatives sell lessons or limit free time, check recitation with AI, or use recorded voice messages. Sanadi gives you:
1. **A real teacher, live.** One tap starts a voice call, just like a phone call.
2. **A teacher of your own gender**, always.
3. **No cost, ever.** No fees, ads or purchases. The teachers volunteer for the sake of Allah.
4. **A design for elders:** big buttons, large text, Arabic and English.
5. **A complete Quran app on its own:** the full mushaf offline, athkar, and Ayah of the day.

**How the listing tells that story**
- **First three screenshots: the core promise.** A real teacher, of your own gender, by phone-style call. Most people decide from the first two or three images.
- **Mixed genders.** Screenshots alternate the brothers' and sisters' versions, so both see themselves and the same-gender matching is visible.
- **The middle screenshots sell the Quran features:** the complete mushaf with night mode, hands-free auto scroll, ayah-by-ayah recitation with a choice of reciters, and athkar for the whole day.
- **The last screenshot recruits teachers.**
- **Search words** (people search these in English and Arabic) appear naturally in the short and full descriptions:
  - English: *Quran, recite, memorize, hifz, tajweed, talqeen, teacher, mushaf, athkar / adhkar, Hisn al-Muslim, Ayah of the day, offline, large text*.
  - Arabic: *القرآن، تسميع، حفظ، تحفيظ، تجويد، تلقين، معلم، مصحف، أذكار الصباح والمساء، حصن المسلم، آية اليوم، دون إنترنت، خط كبير*.
- **Arabic text is written without vowel marks**, except the brand name, so it matches what people type.
- **Arabic addresses both genders.** It uses the usual general form and names brothers and sisters (معلمون ومعلمات) wherever it matters.

**Google Play rules this listing follows**
- **Title and graphics:** no "best", "#1", "top", "new" or other ranking words. No price words ("free") in the title, icon or graphics. No emojis.
- **Descriptions:** "free" appears only in the full description, as a plain fact.
- **Real screens only:** every screenshot is captured from the real app, with no features that don't exist.
- **Honest claims:** no "first" or "only". Teachers are described as reviewed by our team, not as certified.

## Store name

- **English:** "Sanadi Quran: Learn & Memorize". This uses all 30 characters and matches the searches "learn Quran" and "memorize Quran".

- **Brand first:** the name always starts with the brand, so people remember it.
- **Under the app icon on the phone:** it stays short ("Sanadi Quran" / سَنَدي). That name is set in the app, not on Play.
