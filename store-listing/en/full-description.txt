Recite the Quran to a real teacher, by voice call, from home.

Sanadi connects you with volunteer Quran teachers who listen to your recitation and gently correct it, in a simple voice call that works just like a phone call. Brothers recite to brothers, and sisters recite to sisters.

Sanadi is free: no fees, no ads and no in-app purchases. Our teachers give their time for the sake of Allah.

MADE FOR EVERY AGE, ESPECIALLY OUR ELDERS
Big buttons, large clear text and simple screens, in Arabic and English. If you can make a phone call, you can use Sanadi.

RECITE TO A TEACHER
• Tap "Recite now" and we call an available teacher for you
• Teachers of your own gender only
• For memorizing (hifz), review, correcting your recitation and tajweed, or learning to read (talqeen)
• Pick a teacher you like, save favorites, or call whoever is free
• After the lesson, your teacher can leave notes, mark ayahs to practice and set your next portion
• Text messages and voice notes with your teacher
• Calls are never recorded

THE COMPLETE MUSHAF, WORKS OFFLINE
• All 604 pages of the Madani mushaf, in the Hafs script of the King Fahd Complex
• Large text mode for easy reading, with optional auto scroll
• Ayah-by-ayah recitation by well-known reciters (audio needs internet)
• Bookmarks, surah search, and quick jumps to any page or juz

ATHKAR AND AYAH OF THE DAY
• Morning, evening and daily athkar (adhkar) from Hisn al-Muslim, with a counter
• Gentle reminders at the times you choose
• An ayah for everyday life each day, with the Saheeh International translation

FOLLOW YOUR PROGRESS
• Your lessons, minutes and the juz you have recited in
• Your next portion, always one tap away

TEACH FOR THE SAKE OF ALLAH
Do you recite the Quran well? Apply inside the app to volunteer as a teacher and send a short recitation sample. Our team reviews every application. Once you're approved, switch on "available" whenever you're free, and students can call you.

SAFE AND PRIVATE
• Sign in with Google, no passwords
• Same-gender matching
• Report and block tools, reviewed by our admins
• Your phone number is never shown to other users
• Delete your account at any time

Accounts are for adults. Children can recite on a parent's account, with the parent present.

Sanadi (سَنَدي) means "my support". May it support you in learning the Book of Allah.
